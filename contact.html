Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does a demo take?

Most demos take 30 to 45 minutes over a video call. We tailor each one to your organisation and leave plenty of time for questions.

Can you move our existing data into Intelyx?

Yes. We help import your existing records from spreadsheets or other systems as part of onboarding, so you don't start from scratch.

Can the software be customised for us?

Every product can be configured with your own fields, workflows, roles and reports. For larger needs, we also offer custom development.

Do you provide training and support?

Yes. Training for your team is included with every implementation, and our Bengaluru-based support team is available by phone, email and WhatsApp during business hours.
